Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity remained at low levels with C-class flares observed.
Region 4568 (S08W67, Axx/alpha) produced a C4.4 flare at 14/0606 UTC,
the largest of the period. Region 4576 (S17W39, Dai/beta-gamma) produced
a C2.2 flare at 14/1845 UTC. That was followed by Region 4579 (N15E72,
Hsx/alpha), which produced a C2.8/Sf at 14/2127. This flare was
accompanied by a Type II radio sweep, with an estimated velocity of
1,972 km/s, and a Type IV radio sweep. Regions 4574 (N16E20, Dso/beta),
4576, 4577 (S06E04, Dso/beta), and 4579 all experienced growth during
the period, while the remaining regions were stable or in decay.

No Earth-directed CMEs were observed in available coronagraph imagery.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be low with a chance for M-class flares
(R1-R2, Minor-Moderate) over 15-17 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels with a peak
flux of 5,837 pfu observed at 14/1300 UTC. The greater than 10 MeV
proton flux was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to reach high levels
through 16 Nov and decrease to moderate levels on 17 Nov. The greater
than 10 MeV proton flux is expected to continue at background levels on
15-17 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected a near background solar wind regime.
Total field ranged from 4-6 nT while the Bz component was variable
between +/-4 nT. Solar wind speed was mainly between 350-400 km/s until
~14/1100 UTC when it saw a slight increase to around 430 km/s. Phi was
in a mostly negative orientation.

.Forecast...
Nominal solar wind conditions are expected on 15 Nov. A gradual increase
in solar wind parameters is expected on 16 Nov due to an SSBC in advance
of a positive polarity CH HSS, with a further increase on 17 Nov under
effects of a positive polarity CH HSS.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic field was quiet.

.Forecast...
Mostly quiet conditions are expected on 15 Nov, with an increase to
unsettled to isolated active periods on 16 Nov, and a further increase
to active to isolated G1 (Minor) levels on 17 Nov as the CH HSS moves
into a geoeffective position.
