Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity continued at very low levels with only B-level
enhancements observed. Region 4579 (N15W36, Hsx/alpha) remained stable
and unchanged this period. Region 4581 (S08W01, Dro/beta) continued to
exhibited minor decay, losing additional trailing spots.

No Earth-directed CMEs were observed in available coronagraph imagery.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be at very low levels, with a chance for
C-class flares on 23-25 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux remained at moderate levels. The
greater than 10 MeV proton flux continued at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to be at normal to
moderate levels on 23-25 Nov. The greater than 10 MeV proton flux is
expected to continue at background levels on through 25 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters continued near background levels. Total field was
between 1-5 nT, Bz was between +/- 4 nT, and wind speeds decreased from
beginning speeds near 400 km/s to end the period around 320 km/s. Phi
began the period in a negative orientation, switched to the positive
sector after 22/1830 UTC, then rotated back to a negative position at
around 23/1000 UTC.

.Forecast...
Slightly enhanced solar wind conditions are possible on 23 Nov due to
the anticipated glancing influence from the 19 Nov CME. Ambient solar
wind conditions are expected to prevail on 24-25 Nov.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
Geomagnetic field activity was at quiet levels throughout the period.

.Forecast...
The geomagnetic field is expected to be at mostly quiet to unsettled
levels, with isolated active periods likely, on 23 Nov due to possible
glancing CME influences. Mostly quiet conditions are expected to prevail
on 24-25 Nov.
