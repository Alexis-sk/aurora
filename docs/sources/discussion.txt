Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity was at low levels. Newly numbered Region 4584 (N10W15,
Dso/beta) emerged recently but has yet to produced anything above
B-class activity. Region 4585 was responsible for an impulsive C3.9/2n
flare at 27/1849 UTC, the strongest of the period.

No Earth-directed CMEs were observed in available coronagraph imagery.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be very low with a chance for C-flare
activity on 28-30 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux was at normal to moderate levels.
The greater than 10 MeV proton flux was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to be at normal to
moderate levels on 28-29 Nov, with high levels likely on 30 Nov. The
proton flux is expected to remain at background levels through 30 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters continued at mildly enhanced levels. Total field
strength observed a peak of 15 nT, and the Bz component reached as far
south as -11 nT. Solar wind speeds steadily increased from a low of 293
km/s to a peak of ~460 km/s. Phi was variable.

.Forecast...
The solar wind environment is continue at enhanced levels due to
positive polarity CH HSS influences on 28-29 Nov. Background solar wind
conditions are expected to prevail on 30 Nov.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic field was at quiet to active levels.

.Forecast...
The geomagnetic field is expected to be at quiet to active levels on 28
Nov, and quiet to unsettled on 29 Nov, due to positive polarity CH HSS
influences. Quiet levels are expected to prevail on 30 Nov with the
return of ambient solar wind conditions.
