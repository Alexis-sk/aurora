Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity continued at very low levels with only minor B-level
enhancements. Regions 4579 (N15W62, Hsx/alpha) and 4581 (S07W34,
Hrx/alpha) were stable, while newly numbered Region 4582 (N19E52,
Cro/beta) continued to develop its trailing spots.

No Earth-directed CMEs were observed in available coronagraph imagery.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be at very low levels, with a slight
chance for C-class flares on 25-27 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux remained at moderate levels. The
greater than 10 MeV proton flux continued at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to be at normal to
moderate levels on 25-27 Nov. The greater than 10 MeV proton flux is
expected to continue at background levels through 27 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters continued at background levels. Total magnetic
field (Bt) was between 1-6 nT, with a Bz component was between -5/+6 nT.
Solar wind speeds averaged roughly 320 km/s throughout the reporting
period. The phi angle indicated a boundary crossing at approximately
24/0300 UTC (negative to positive) and another at approximately 24/1400
UTC (positive to negative), with a return to generally positive
orientation after approximately 24/1700 UTC.

.Forecast...
Ambient solar wind conditions are expected to prevail on 25 Nov and into
26 Nov. A recurrent positive polarity coronal hole high speed stream (CH
HSS) may begin onset late on 26 Nov or 27 Nov, bringing enhanced solar
wind conditions.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
Geomagnetic field activity was at quiet levels throughout the period.

.Forecast...
Mostly quiet conditions are expected to prevail on 25 Nov, with isolated
active periods possible late on 26 Nov from a possible positive polarity
CH HSS onset. Isolated G1 (Minor) storming conditions are anticipated on
27 Nov as the CH HSS fully arrives.
