Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity returned to low levels, with the largest flare of the
reporting period being a C7.3 at 18/0037 UTC from Region 4579 (N16E18,
Hkx/alpha), which developed a distinct asymmetry throughout the period.
Region 4578 (S11W86, Eai/beta-gamma) also produced a C6.6 at 18/0620
UTC. Region 4581 (S08E53, Dai/beta-gamma) continued to exhibit extensive
flux emergence in its intermediary region. The remaining active regions
appeared either stable or in slight decay.

A CME associated with the M1.7 at 17/2130 UTC from Region 4578 finally
had enough imagery to be analyzed; modeling indicates that like the CME
associated with the earlier M1.1 from the same region, the structure is
likely to become entangled with the CME structures ahead of it. A large
structure became visible in LASCO C2 imagery at approximately at 18/0125
UTC and in STEREO coronagraph imagery at approximately 18/0525 UTC, with
field line movement but no X-ray flare seen in Region 4578 around that
an hour earlier. However, data gaps and contamination made analysis
extremely difficult at the time of the eruption and a full analysis was
only able to be carried out this reporting period. Triangulation with
STEREO indicates that only the southern half of the structure is from
the Earth-facing side of the western limb, and modeling suggests this
structure will also become entangled with the previous CMEs. Both of
these structures are likely to contribute to a potential glancing blow
from the filament eruption that left the Sun on 14 Nov, arriving at the
near-Earth environment mid-day on 22 Nov.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be low with a chance for M-class flares
(R1-R2, Minor-Moderate) on 19 Nov. Probabilities for M-class events
decrease to a slight chance on 20-21 Nov as Region 4578 exits the
western limb.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels with a peak
flux of 1,004 pfu observed at 18/1630 UTC. The greater than 10 MeV
proton flux was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to reach high levels on
19-21 Nov. The greater than 10 MeV proton flux is expected to continue
at background levels on through 21 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected waning influence from a minor positive
polarity CH HSS. Total magnetic field (Bt) gradually decreased from 10
nT at the beginning of the period to end at approximately 7 nT. The Bz
(north-south) component was largely southward, with several deflections
of -6 to -9 nT throughout the period.

Solar wind speeds averaged approximately 375 km/s until roughly 18/2020
UTC, when they began to increase, ending the period at 415 km/s level.
Phi was positive (away from the Sun), except for a roughly 6 hr interval
starting at approximately 18/1240 UTC.

.Forecast...
Solar wind parameters are expected to continue trending towards
background levels on 19 Nov as the influence of the weak positive
polarity CH HSS continues to wane. A solar sector boundary crossing
anticipated on 20 Nov and the potential onset of a weak negative
polarity CH HSS on 21 Nov are likely to elevated the solar wind
parameters on those days.

Largely unsettled conditions are anticipated on 19-21 Nov.
