Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity continued at moderate levels. Region 4566 (S06W07,
Dkc/beta-gamma-delta) produced three M-class flares (R1-Minor), the
largest an M1.7 at 08/0746 UTC, along with frequent C-class events this
period. The region added penumbral area around its intermediate and
trailer spots. Region 4568 (S08WE13, Bxo/beta) reemerged but remained
relatively simple. The remaining numbered regions were either stable or
in decay.

No Earth-directed CMEs were observed in available coronagraph imagery.

.Forecast...
M-class flares (R1-R2/Minor-Moderate) are likely, with a slight chance
for an X-class flare (R3-Strong), over 09-11 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels. The greater
than 10 MeV proton flux was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is likely to reached high levels
over 09-11 Nov. There is a slight chance for the greater than 10 MeV
proton flux to exceed S1 (Minor) levels over 09-11 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected the influence from a negative polarity
CH HSS. Total magnetic field strength reached 10 nT, and the Bz
component reached as far south as -9 nT. Solar wind speeds remained
elevated through the day with a peak values around 814 km/s. Phi was
mostly oriented in the negative solar sector.

.Forecast...
The solar wind environment is expected to remain enhanced as CH HSS
influences continue over 09-11 Nov.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic field was at unsettled to G1 (Minor) storm levels under
continued negative polarity CH HSS influences.

.Forecast...
The geomagnetic field is likely to reach G1 (Minor) geomagnetic storm
levels on 09 Nov due to continued negative polarity CH HSS influences.
Active conditions on 10 Nov and unsettled conditions are 11 Nov are
likely as coronal hole effects wane.
