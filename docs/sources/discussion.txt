Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity continued at very low levels with only B-level
enhancements. Region 4581 (S10W29, Hrx/alpha) underwent significant
decay, losing its trailing spots. Region 4582 (N18E54, Cro/beta) emerged
during the period, was subsequently numbered, but remained quiet. No
Earth-directed CMEs were observed in available coronagraph imagery.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be at very low levels, with a slight
chance for C-class flares on 24-26 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux remained at moderate levels. The
greater than 10 MeV proton flux continued at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to be at normal to
moderate levels on 24-26 Nov. The greater than 10 MeV proton flux is
expected to continue at background levels on through 26 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters continued at background levels. Total magnetic
field (Bt) was between 1-5 nT, with a Bz component between +/- 4 nT.
Solar wind speeds averaged roughly 320 km/s throughout the reporting
period. Phi became predominantly positive after 24/0255 UTC.

.Forecast...
Ambient solar wind conditions are expected to prevail on 24-25 Nov and
into 26 Nov. A positive polarity coronal hole high speed stream (CH HSS)
may begin onset late on 26 Nov, bringing enhanced solar wind conditions.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
Geomagnetic field activity was at quiet levels throughout the period.

.Forecast...
Mostly quiet conditions are expected to prevail on 24-25 Nov, with
isolated active periods possible late on 26 Nov due to positive polarity
CH HSS onset.
