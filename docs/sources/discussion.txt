Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity continued at very low levels with only B-level
enhancements observed. Two new regions of emerging flux were noted in
the E. hemisphere and will be assigned active region numbers if they
persist. No Earth-directed CMEs were observed in available coronagraph
imagery.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be at very low levels, with a slight
chance for C-class flares on 27-29 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux remained at moderate levels. The
greater than 10 MeV proton flux continued at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to be at normal to
moderate levels on 27-28 Nov, with high levels likely by 29 Nov. The
greater than 10 MeV proton flux is expected to continue at background
levels through 29 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters transitioned from nominal conditions to mildly
enhanced after 27/0530 UTC. A steady increase in total magnetic field
strength, up to a peak of ~13 nT at 27/1118 UTC. No significant periods
of Bz south were observed. Solar wind speeds remained low, with speeds
ranging from 276-346 km/s. Phi angle was predominantly oriented in the
positive solar sector.

.Forecast...
Enhanced solar wind conditions due to positive polarity CH HSS
influences are expected on 27-28 Nov, with background levels likely to
return by 29 Nov.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
Geomagnetic field activity was quiet.

.Forecast...
Isolated G1 (Minor) geomagnetic storms are likely on 27 Nov and
unsettled to active conditions on 28 Nov due to the positive polarity CH
HSS moving through near-Earth space. 29 Nov is expected to see a
returned to quiet conditions.
