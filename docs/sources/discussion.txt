Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity reached moderate levels due to an M1.7 flare at 11/2121
UTC from Region 4566 (S07W48, Eki/beta-gamma). Region 4566 continued to
decay, decreasing in both areal coverage and magnetic complexity. Slight
growth was observed in Region 4574 (N16E61, Dao/beta). The rest of the
numbered regions were either stable or in decay.

No Earth-directed CMEs were observed in available coronagraph imagery.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be low with a chance for M-class flares
(R1-R2, Minor-Moderate) over 12-14 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels with a peak
flux of 7,897 pfu observed at 11/1555 UTC. The greater than 10 MeV
proton flux was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is likely to reach high levels over
12-14 Nov. The greater than 10 MeV proton flux is expected to continue
at background levels on 12-14 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected continued, but diminishing influence,
from a negative polarity CH HSS. Total field ranged from 1-5 nT while
the Bz component was between +4/-3 nT. Solar wind speed decreased from
approximately 555 km/s to near 485 km/s. Phi angle was mostly negative
with some variability after 11/0955 UTC.

.Forecast...
The solar wind environment is expected to gradually diminish on 12 Nov.
A return to nominal conditions is expected by 13-14 Nov.
