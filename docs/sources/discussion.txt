Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity reached moderate levels with two M-flares from Region
4578 (S11W73, Dri/beta-gamma): an M1.1 at 17/1549 UTC and an M1.7 at
17/2139 UTC. Region 4572 (N13W44, Cai/beta) grew in overall extent while
gaining new spots primarily in its intermediate area. Newly numbered
Region 4581 (S08E59, Dai/beta) underwent evolution, exhibiting rapid
flux emergence while developing asymmetric penumbra on both its leading
and trailing spot groups while producing low-level C-class flaring. The
remaining active regions appeared either stable or in slight decay.

A CME associated with the M1.1 first became visible in LASCO C2 imagery
at approximately 17/1600 UTC. Modeling indicates that this eruption
becomes tangled with the CMEs from 16 Nov and that the structure may
produce a glancing blow early on 22 Nov. An eruption near Region 4579
was observed by SUVI around 17/1930 UTC with the associated CME being
determined to be off the Sun-Earth Line. Multiple westerly CMEs
originating from either on or just beyond the western limb were observed
after 18/0000 UTC and are thus not currently considered a significant
Earth-directed threat.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be low with a chance for M-class flares
(R1-R2, Minor-Moderate) over 18-19 Nov. Probabilities decrease to a
slight chance thereafter as Region 4578 exits the western limb.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels with a peak
flux of 3,668 pfu observed at 17/1710 UTC. The greater than 10 MeV
proton flux was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to reach moderate
levels on 18 Nov, with an anticipated return to high levels on 19-20
Nov. The greater than 10 MeV proton flux is expected to continue at
background levels on through 20 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters were weakly enhanced under CIR effects associated
with a positive polarity CH HSS. Total field increased from 7 nT to a
peak of 10 nT while the Bz component underwent several brief southward
deflections reaching -5 to -8 nT. Solar wind speeds increased gradually
from near 300 km/s to just above 400 km/s by the end of the reporting
period. Phi was predominantly positive.

.Forecast...
Weak enhancements in solar wind parameters are likely to continue
through 18 Nov as a positive polarity CH HSS continues to move into a
geoeffective position, coupled with the possible arrival of the 14 Nov
CME. These enhancements are anticipated to begin waning through 19 Nov,
with a solar sector boundary crossing anticipated on 20 Nov.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic field was at quiet to unsettled levels during the
period.

.Forecast...
Active to isolated G1 (Minor) levels are likely on 18 Nov as the CH HSS
continues to move into a geoeffective position along with the possible
arrival of the 14 Nov CME late in the period. Largely unsettled
conditions are anticipated on 19-20 Nov, with a chance for isolated
active periods on 20 Nov due to an anticipated solar sector boundary
crossing.
