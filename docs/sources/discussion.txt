Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity reached low levels. New Region 4584 (S10E68, Cro/beta)
produced all three C-class flares observed this period, the largest
being a C3.9 at 27/1849 UTC. New Region 4583 (N05E33, Cao/beta) produced
a few low-level B-class enhancements, but was otherwise unremarkable. No
Earth-directed CMEs were observed in available coronagraph imagery.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be very low with a chance for C-flare
activity on 28-30 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux was at normal to moderate levels.
The greater than 10 MeV proton flux was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to be at normal to
moderate levels on 28 Nov, with high levels likely on 29-30 Nov. The
proton flux is expected to remain at background levels through 30 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected the initial onset of positive polarity
CH HSS influences. Total field strength increased to 15 nT, and the Bz
component was predominately northward, only briefly reaching -6 nT late
in the period. Solar wind speeds gradually increased from a low of 280
km/s to a peak of around 375 km/s.

.Forecast...
The solar wind environment is expected to remain weakly enhanced due to
positive polarity CH HSS influences on 28-29 Nov. Background solar wind
conditions are expected to prevail on 30 Nov.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic field was at quiet levels.

.Forecast...
The geomagnetic field is expected to be at quiet to active levels on 28
Nov, and quiet to unsettled on 29 Nov, due to positive polarity CH HSS
influences. Quiet levels are expected to prevail on 30 Nov with the
return of ambient solar wind conditions.
