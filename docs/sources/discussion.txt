Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity continued at moderate levels. Region 4566 (S06W00,
Dkc/beta-gamma-delta) produced four M-class flares (R1-Minor), the
largest an M1.9 at 07/1842 UTC, along with numerous C-class events this
period. Continued sunspot development was observed in this regions
intermediate and trailer spots. New Region 4571 (S14W47, Dro/beta)
emerged with spots late in the period, but was otherwise unremarkable.
The remaining numbered regions were either stable or in decay. Other
activity included a DSF centered near ~N35W15 that began at around
08/0050 UTC, though the associated CME(s) are not expected to be
Earth-directed.

No Earth-directed CMEs were observed in available coronagraph imagery.

.Forecast...
M-class flares (R1-R2/Minor-Moderate) are likely, with a slight chance
for an X-class flare (R3-Strong), over 08-10 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux was at moderate levels. The greater
than 10 MeV proton flux was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is likely to be at normal to
moderate levels on 08-09 Nov, with high levels likely by 10 Nov. There
is a slight chance for the greater than 10 MeV proton flux to exceed S1
(Minor) levels over 08-10 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected the influence from a negative polarity
CH HSS. Total magnetic field strength reached 15 nT, and the Bz
component reached as far south as -11 nT. Solar wind speeds increased
steadily from a low of around 500 km/s to a peak of ~763 km/s. Phi was
mostly oriented in the negative solar sector.

.Forecast...
The solar wind environment is expected to remain enhanced as CH HSS
influences continue over 08-10 Nov.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic field was at unsettled to G1 (Minor) storm levels under
continued negative polarity CH HSS influences.

.Forecast...
The geomagnetic field is expected to reach G1 (Minor) geomagnetic storm
levels, with a chance for G2 (Moderate) storm levels, on 08-09 Nov due
to continued negative polarity CH HSS influences. Unsettled to active
conditions are likely on 10 Nov as coronal hole effects wane.
