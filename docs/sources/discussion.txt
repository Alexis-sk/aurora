Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity was at low levels.

Nine numbered regions were on the disk and were mostly stable. Region
4570 (S13E59, Axx/alpha) produced the strongest flare of the period, a
C5.7/Sf at 06/0251 UTC. However, Region 4566 (S06E19,
Dac/beta-gamma-delta) produced most of the low-level C-class X-ray
activity during the UT day. This region was the only one on the visible
disk to display significant growth during the past 24 hours.

No Earth-directed CMEs were observed.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be low with a chance for M-class flares
(R1-R2), and a slight chance for R3 (Strong), over 07-09 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels with a peak
flux of 4631 pfu observed at 06/1720 UTC. The greater than 10 MeV proton
flux was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The electron flux is likely to be at normal to moderate levels over
07-09 Nov due to anticipated goemagnetic activity associated with the
negative polartiy CH HSS. The proton flux is expected to remain at
background.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected a gradual enhancment in the IMF and
solar wind speeds, likely associated with the onset of a influence from
a negative polarity CH HSS. Total magnetic field strength peaked late in
the period with values that reached 13 nT. The Bz compoent briefly
reached as far south as -9 nT but has been predominantly positive during
the past 24 hours. Solar wind speeds steadily increased from 305 km/s to
~422 near the end of the reporting period. Phi was mostly oriented in
the negative solar sector.

.Forecast...
The solar wind environment is expected to remain enhanced as CH HSS
influences continued over 07-09 Nov.
