Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity was at low levels. Region 4578 continued to produce
C-class flare activity as it exited the western limb to include a C4.9
flare at 18/1320 UTC. Region 4576 followed suit producing departing
activity in the form of a C4.9 as well which peaked at 19/0049 UTC.
Region 4581 (S09E45, Dai/beta-gamma) remained noteworthy as it grew in
length, gained additional spots and exhibited maturing penumbra on both
its leading and trailing spots. Despite this growth, AR 4581 only
produced C1 and C2 flares during the reporting period.

Other activity included a minor eruption originating from Region 4579
(N15E11, Hax/alpha) which began at approximately 19/0708 UTC as observed
in GOES SUVI 304 imagery. A possibly related subsequent narrow and faint
CME was then seen in LASCO C2 and STEREO-A coronagraph imagery beginning
near 19/0924 UTC and 19/0753 UTC, respectively. Modeling of this event
was attempted with results pending still pending as of the time of this
writing.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be low with a chance for M-class flares
(R1-R2, Minor-Moderate) on 19 Nov. Probabilities for M-class events
decrease to a slight chance on 20-21 Nov as Region 4578 fully exits the
western limb.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels with a peak
flux of 1,004 pfu observed at one five minute interval at 18/1630 UTC.
The greater than 10 MeV proton flux was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to reach high levels on
19-21 Nov. The greater than 10 MeV proton flux is expected to continue
at background levels on through 21 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected relatively weak positive polarity CH HSS
influences. Total fiend generally ranged 5-9 nT. The Bz component of the
IMF underwent a few sustained southward deflections reaching -5 to - 7
nT, but was otherwise near neutral or northward. Solar wind speeds
increased from ~370-380 km/s to ~425-450 km/s, indicating the onset of
the high speed stream itself at approximately 18/2010 UTC. Phi became
predominantly positive near 18/1844 UTC.

.Forecast...
Sustained positive polarity CH HSS influences are expected to persist
for the remainder of 19 Nov. A solar sector boundary crossing
anticipated on 20 Nov and the potential onset of a weak negative
polarity CH HSS on 21 Nov are likely to cause additional disturbances on
those respective days.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic field was largely quiet to unsettled with the exception
of an isolated G1 (Minor) geomagnetic storm period at the beginning of
the 19 Nov UTC-day.

.Forecast...
Primarily quiet to unsettled conditions are expected through 21 Nov with
a chance for isolated active periods.
