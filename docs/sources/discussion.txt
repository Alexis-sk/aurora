Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity remained at low levels. Region 4578 (S12W67, Dri/beta)
produced a C5.3 at 16/1630 UTC, which was the largest event of the
reporting period. Regions 4572 (N14W32, Cro/beta) and 4577 (S07W30,
Dro/beta) tacked on low level C-class flares as well. New spots centered
near S08E04 were observed, but remain unnumbered this period as we await
corroborating solar observatory reports or signs of significant
activity. No new Earth-directed CMEs were observed this reporting
period.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be low with a slight chance for M-class
flares (R1-R2, Minor-Moderate) over 17-19 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels with a peak
flux of 4,744 pfu observed at 16/1500 UTC. The greater than 10 MeV
proton flux was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to reach moderate
levels on 17-18 Nov, with an anticipated return to high levels on 19
Nov. The greater than 10 MeV proton flux is expected to continue at
background levels on 17-19 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
The solar wind environment reflected a minor disturbance beginning near
the turn of the UTC-day which appeared to have resulted in a solar
sector boundary crossing. Total field was mostly ambient-like until late
in the period where a sharp enhancement occurred near 17/0730 UTC and
values increased to 7 nT. The Bz component also deflected southward at
this time, reaching -4 nT. However, solar wind speeds remained
relatively low averaging near 300 km/s. Phi became positive at
approximately 17/0732 UTC.

.Forecast...
Weak enhancements in solar wind parameters are likely to continue over
the course of 17 Nov and through 18 Nov as a positive polarity CH HSS
moves into a geoeffective position, coupled with the possible arrival of
the 14 Nov CME late on 17 Nov into early 18 Nov. These enhancements are
anticipated to begin waning through 19 Nov.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic field was quiet.

.Forecast...
Active to isolated G1 (Minor) levels are likely on 17-18 Nov as the CH
HSS moves into a geoeffective position along with the possible arrival
of the 14 Nov CME late in the period. Largely unsettled conditions are
anticipated on 19 Nov.
