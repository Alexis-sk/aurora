Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity continued at moderate levels. Region 4566 (S07W13,
Dkc/beta-gamma-delta) produced two M-class flares (R1-Minor), the
largest an M1.1 at 08/1827 UTC, along with numerous C-class events this
period as the region began to show signs of decay. New Region 4572
(N13E76, Hsx/alpha) was numbered, but its proximity to the limb
precluded accurate analysis. The remaining numbered regions were either
stable or in decay.

No Earth-directed CMEs were observed in available coronagraph imagery.

.Forecast...
M-class flares (R1-R2/Minor-Moderate) are likely, with a slight chance
for an X-class flare (R3-Strong), over 09-11 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels. The greater
than 10 MeV proton flux was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to reach high levels on
09-11 Nov. There is a slight chance for the greater than 10 MeV proton
flux to exceed S1 (Minor) levels over 09-11 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected the influence from a negative polarity
CH HSS. Total magnetic field strength reached 6 nT, and the Bz component
reached as far south as -5 nT. Solar wind speeds ranged between 690-814
km/s throughout the period. Phi was mostly oriented in the negative
solar sector.

.Forecast...
The solar wind environment is expected to remain enhanced as CH HSS
influences continue over 09-11 Nov.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic field was at unsettled to active levels under continued
negative polarity CH HSS influences.

.Forecast...
The geomagnetic field is likely to reach G1 (Minor) geomagnetic storm
levels on 09 Nov due to continued negative polarity CH HSS influences.
Periods of active conditions are likely on 10 Nov, and quiet to
unsettled conditions are likely on 11 Nov, as coronal hole influences
wane.
