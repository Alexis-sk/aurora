Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity dropped to very low levels with only B-level enhancements
observed. Region 4579 (N14W16, Cso/beta) added a few trailer spots and
was the only region to show growth during the period. Region 4581
(S07E18, Dso/beta) exhibited overall decay, while the remaining regions
decayed to plage as they rotated towards the west limb.

No Earth-directed CMEs were identified in the available coronagraph
imagery during the period.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is likely to be at low levels, with a slight chance for
M-class flares (R1-R2, Minor-Moderate) on 21-23 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux remained at moderate levels. The
greater than 10 MeV proton flux continued at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to be at moderate
levels on 21-22 Nov, reaching high levels on 23 Nov. The greater than 10
MeV proton flux is expected to continue at background levels on through
23 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters were indicative of a nominal solar wind regime.
Total interplanetary magnetic field (Bt) averaged around 5 nT, the Bz
component fluctuated between +/- 5 nT, and solar wind speeds decreased
further, from ~385 km/s down to ~345 km/s. Phi was positive through the
whole reported period.

.Forecast...
Continued background solar wind conditions are likely on 21-22 Nov.
Slight enhancements are possible on 23 Nov due to a low confidence
glancing blow of CMEs from 14 Nov and 19 Nov.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic field has been at mostly quiet levels, with an isolated
unsettled period during the 21/0000-0003 UTC synoptic period.

.Forecast...
Quiet to unsettled conditions are expected on 21-22 Nov. Quiet to active
periods are expected on 22 Nov, with a slight chance for isolated G1
(Minor) storming period due to the possible CME(s) glancing influences.
