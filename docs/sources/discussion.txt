Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity continued at low levels. Regions 4575 (S10E12,
Hsx/alpha), 4578 (S12W46, Cso/beta) and 4579 (N15E58, Hsx/alpha) all
produced weak C-class flares during the period. Regions 4574 (N17E06,
Dai/beta) and 4578 exhibited growth, mainly in their leader spots. The
remaining regions were stable or in decay.

From approximately 14/0130-0930 UTC, a large filament was observed in
GOES SUVI/AIA 304 imagery lifting off the solar disk in the southern
hemisphere. However, no discernible CME could be located in available
coronagraph imagery.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be low with a chance for M-class flares
(R1-R2, Minor-Moderate) over 16-18 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels with a peak
flux of 3,378 pfu observed at 15/1405 UTC. The greater than 10 MeV
proton flux was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to reach high levels on
16 Nov and decrease to moderate levels on 17-18 Nov. The greater than 10
MeV proton flux is expected to continue at background levels on 16-18
Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected ambient solar wind conditions. Total
field ranged from 2-4 nT while the Bz component was variable between
+/-3 nT. Solar wind speed saw a slow, gradual decrease from beginning
values near 400 km/s to end the period around 340 km/s. Phi was in a
mostly negative orientation.

.Forecast...
Nominal solar wind conditions are expected early on 16 Nov, with a
gradual increase in solar wind parameters later on 16 Nov. This increase
is due to the anticipated onset of a SSBC in advance of a positive
polarity CH HSS. Further enhancements are expected on 17-18 Nov as a
positive polarity CH HSS moves into a geoeffective position. This is
coupled with the possible arrival of the 14 Nov CME late on 17 Nov into
early 18 Nov.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic field was quiet.

.Forecast...
Mostly quiet conditions are expected early on 16 Nov, increasing to
unsettled to isolated active periods later on 16 Nov. A further increase
to active to isolated G1 (Minor) levels on 17-18 Nov as the CH HSS moves
into a geoeffective position. This is coupled with the possible arrival
of the 14 Nov CME late in the period.
