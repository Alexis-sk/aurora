Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity increased to moderate levels. Region 4566 (S06E06,
Dkc/beta-gamma-delta) was the primary producer of X-ray activity during
the period. Three R1 (Minor) events were observed, the largest of which
was an M1.9 (R1) flare at 07/1842 UTC. Continued sunspot development was
observed in this regions intermediate and trailer spots, while the
remaining numbered regions were either stable or in decay.

Despite the elevated flare activity, no Earth-directed CMEs were
observed in available coronagraph imagery.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is likely to see moderate M-class flares
(R1-R2/Minor-Moderate), and a chance for an X-class flare (R3-Strong)
over 08-10 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux decreased to moderate levels. The
greater than 10 MeV proton flux was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is likely to be at normal to
moderate levels on 08-09 Nov, with high levels likely by 10 Nov. There
is a slight chance for the greater than 10 MeV proton flux to exceed S1
(Minor) levels over 08-10 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected the influence from a negative polarity
CH HSS. Total magnetic field strength reached 15 nT, and the Bz
component reached as far south as -11 nT. Solar wind speeds increased
steadily from a low of around 425 km/s to a peak of ~736 km/s. Phi was
mostly oriented in the negative solar sector.

.Forecast...
The solar wind environment is expected to remain enhanced as CH HSS
influences continues over 08-10 Nov.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic field reached G1 (Minor) geomagnetic storm levels late
in the UT day.

.Forecast...
The geomagnetic field is expected to reach G1 (Minor) geomagnetic storm
levels, with a chance for G2 (Moderate) storm levels on 08 Nov, likely
continuing into 09 Nov due to anticipated influence from a negative
polarity CH HSS. As coronal hole effects wane, unsettled to active
conditions are likely on 10 Nov.
