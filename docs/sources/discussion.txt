Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity remained at low levels with C-class flares observed.
Newly numbered Region 4579 (N15E79, Hsx/alpha) produced a C5.5 flare at
13/1821 UTC, the largest of the period, as well as the majority of the
other C-flares observed. Region 4568 (S08W60, Axx/alpha) added a C4.4
flare at 14/0606 UTC, while a few other regions contributed smaller
C-flares during the period. Regions 4574 (N16E27, Dso/beta), 4576
(S17W30, Dai/beta-gamma), and 4577 (S06E11, Dso/beta) all experienced
growth during the period, while the remaining regions were stable or in
decay.

No Earth-directed CMEs were observed in available coronagraph imagery.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be low with a chance for M-class flares
(R1-R2, Minor-Moderate) over 14-16 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels with a peak
flux of 5,223 pfu observed at 13/1700 UTC. The greater than 10 MeV
proton flux was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to reach high levels
through 16 Nov. The greater than 10 MeV proton flux is expected to
continue at background levels on 14-16 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected a near background solar wind regime.
Total field ranged from 3-6 nT while the Bz component was variable
between +/-4 nT. Solar wind speed was mainly between 350-400 km/s until
~14/1100 UTC when it saw a slight increase to around 430 km/s. Phi was
in a mostly negative orientation.

.Forecast...
Nominal solar wind conditions are expected on 14-15 Nov. A gradual
increase in solar wind parameters is expected on 16 Nov due to an SSBC
in advance of a positive polarity CH HSS.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic field was quiet.

.Forecast...
Mostly quiet conditions are expected on 14-15 Nov, with an increase to
unsettled to isolated active periods on 16 Nov as the CH HSS moves into
a geoeffective position.
