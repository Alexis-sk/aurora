Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity reached low levels. Region 4564 (N04E24, Cro/beta)
produced a C1.1 flare at 01/1236 UTC and exhibited minor decay. Region
4565 (N13E42, Hsx/alpha) was stable and quiet throughout the period. New
Region 4566 (S08E75, Cao/beta) was numbered as it rotated into view from
the SE limb, but the region remained quiet. No Earth-directed CMEs were
observed.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be very low with a chance for C-class
flares over 02-04 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected waning positive polarity CH HSS
influences. Total field strength reached 6 nT and the Bz component
reached as far south as -4 nT. Solar wind speeds slowly decreased from a
peak of around 500 km/s early in the period to between 400-450 km/s.

.Forecast...
The solar wind environment is expected to remain mildly enhanced on 02
Nov as positive polarity CH HSS influences diminish. Background solar
wind conditions are expected to prevail over 03-04 Nov.
