Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity was at low levels. Region 4566 (S07W34,
Ekc/beta-gamma-delta) produced the majority of the C-class flares. The
largest was a C9.3 at 10/0834 UTC. Region 4566 displayed overall
indications of weakening as the spot group decays, although the region
still contains several small delta magnetic areas. Slight growth was
observed in Regions 4568 (S09W15, Cso/beta) and newly numbered 4573
(N08E50, Dro/beta).

No Earth-directed CMEs were observed in available coronagraph imagery.

.Forecast...
M-class flares (R1-R2/Minor-Moderate) are likely, with a slight chance
for an X-class flare (R3-Strong), over 11-13 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels with a peak
flux of 6,420 pfu observed at 10/1550 UTC. The greater than 10 MeV
proton flux was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is likely to reach high levels over
11-13 Nov. There is a slight chance for the greater than 10 MeV proton
flux to exceed S1 (Minor) levels over 11-13 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected continued, but diminishing influence,
from a negative polarity CH HSS. Total field ranged from 2-5 nT while
the Bz component was between +5/-3 nT. Solar wind speed decreased from
approximately 705 km/s to near 600 km/s. Phi angle was mostly negative.

.Forecast...
The solar wind environment is expected to gradually diminish on 11-12
Nov. A return to nominal conditions is expected by 13 Nov.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic field was quiet to unsettled.

.Forecast...
Quiet to unsettled conditions are expected on 11-12 Nov as HSS activity
gradually diminishes. Mostly quiet conditions are expected to return on
13 Nov.
