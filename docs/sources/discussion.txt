Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity continued at very low levels. Region 4564 (N04E65,
Bxo/beta) developed trailer spots this period. A new region began
rotating onto the disk; the region will be evaluated for numbering once
it has rotated past the worst of the limb foreshortening effects.

No Earth-directed CMEs were observed at the available coronagraph
imagery.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be at very low levels with a slight chance
for isolated C-class flares through 01 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels, with a peak
flux of 1,228 pfu observed at 29/1630 UTC. The greater than 10 MeV
proton flux remained steady at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to decrease to normal
to moderate levels on 30 Oct Oct and remain there through 01 Nov. The
greater than 10 MeV proton flux is anticipated to persist at background
levels through 01 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected generally undisturbed conditions near
Earth in the first half of the period, with the wind speeds less than
350 km/s, a total magnetic field (Bt) averaging 4 nT, and a largely
neutral or north Bz (north-south) component. After approximately 30/0500
UTC, a magnetic structure began moving through: Bt reached a max of 9 nT
at 30/0700 UTC while Bz reached a max southward deflection of -6 nT at
30/0615 UTC. Solar wind speeds had a very slight increase but remained
below 350 km/s. The phi angle was largely negative (towards the Sun),
except for a 5hr interval after 30/0500 UTC which crossed into the
positive sector, potentially indicated a sector boundary crossing ahead
of the anticipated CH HSS.

.Forecast...
Solar wind disturbances are likely to occur late on 30 Oct, continuing
into 31 Oct - 01 Nov, due to the anticipated arrival of a recurrent,
equatorial positive polarity CH HSS.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic field was quiet during the period.

.Forecast...
Unsettled to active geomagnetic levels are expected late on 30 Oct as
the positive polarity CH HSS effects begin, with G1 (Minor) geomagnetic
storming becoming likely on 31 Oct and 01 Nov.
