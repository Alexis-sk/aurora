Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity remained at very low levels with only a few B-class
flares observed in the past 24h. The largest event was a B5.3 at 21/0708
UTC that originated from Region 4577 (S06, Lo=57). This region decayed
to plage during the period but remained active as it rotated towards the
west limb. There are only two active regions with spots on the visible
disk: Region 4579 (N15W18, Cso/beta) and Region 4581 (S07E12, Dso/beta).
Region 4579 showed some decay in the past hours. Region 4572 (N12W94,
Axx/alpha) rotated off during the reported period.

No Earth-directed CMEs were identified in the available coronagraph
imagery during the period.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is likely to reach low levels, with a slight chance for
M-class flares (R1-R2, Minor-Moderate) on 22-24 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux remained at moderate levels. The
greater than 10 MeV proton flux continued at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to be at moderate
levels on 22 Nov, reaching high levels on 23-24 Nov. The greater than 10
MeV proton flux is expected to continue at background levels on through
24 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters indicated an undisturbed solar wind regime, with
total interplanetary magnetic field (Bt) around 5 nT and Bz component
oscillating between +/- 5 nT. The solar wind speed was about 350 km/s
(+/- 25 km/s) during the period. Phi was predominately positive through
the reported period.

.Forecast...
Nominal solar wind conditions are likely on 22 and 24 Nov, with a slight
and short-lived disturbance possible on 23 Nov due to a low confidence
glancing blow of CMEs from 14 Nov and 19 Nov.
