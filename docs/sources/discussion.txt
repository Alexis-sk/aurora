Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity reached moderate levels. Region 4566 (S06E28,
Fki/beta-gamma-delta) produced an M3.5/2b flare at 05/1833 UTC.
Associated with the event was a Type II radio sweep (est. shock speed of
609 km/s reported by Sag Hill Observatory). Subsequent coronagraph
imagery contained the intial signature of a CME but further coronagraph
imagery is needed to fully analyze the event.

Newly numbered Region 4568 (S08E53, Bxi/beta) was mostly quiet as it
developed from plage. Another region of flux was observed rotating
around the SE limb but was not developed enough to assign an active
region number. Only minor changes were observed in the remaining spotted
active regions on the visible disk.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be at low levels, with a chance for
M-class flares and a slight chance for X-class events on 06-08 Nov,
primarily due to the potential from Regions 4565 and 4566.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux continued to reach high levels with
a maximum flux of 2,197 pfu observed at 05/1700 UTC. The greater than 10
MeV proton flux remained at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to continue reaching
high levels on 06 Nov, returning to moderate levels on 07-08 Nov. The
greater than 10 MeV proton flux is expected to remain at background
levels through 08 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected nominal conditions. Total magnetic field
strength reached 7 nT. No significant periods of southward Bz were
observed. Solar wind speeds declined from ~380 km/s to ~320 km/s by the
end of the UT day. Phi was predominantly oriented in a positive solar
sector.

.Forecast...
The solar wind disturbances are likely on 06-08 Nov due to the
anticipated influence of a negative polarity CH HSS over the next three
days (06-08 Nov).

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic field was quiet.

.Forecast...
The geomagnetic field activity is likely to reach G1 (Minor) geomagnetic
storm levels on 06-08 Nov, with a chance for G2 (Moderate) levels on
07-08 Nov, due to the anticipated influences of a negative polarity CH
HSS.
