Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity continued at very low levels with only B-level
enhancements. There are three numbered regions on the disk, and all of
them were stabled throughout the reporting period. No Earth-directed
CMEs were observed in available coronagraph imagery.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be at very low levels, with a slight
chance for C-class flares on 26-28 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux remained at moderate levels. The
greater than 10 MeV proton flux continued at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to be at normal to
moderate levels on 26-27 Nov, and then reaching high levels on 28 Nov.
The greater than 10 MeV proton flux is expected to continue at
background levels through 28 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected solar sector boundary crossings and
possibly early CIR influences. Total magnetic field (Bt) ranged from 2
to 8 nT, while the Bz component ranged from +/- 6 nT. Solar wind speeds
underwent a slight increase from ~310 km/s to ~395 km/s, but decreased
to ~350 km/s by the end of the reporting period. Phi angle was mostly
positive with deviations into a negative sector between 25/0735-1632
UTC.

.Forecast...
Slightly disturbed solar wind conditions are anticipated to continue
through late on 26 Nov as a recurrent positive polarity coronal hole
high speed stream (CH HSS) becomes geoeffective bringing fast solar
wind. CH HSS influences are expected to then persist through 28 Nov.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
Geomagnetic field activity was at quiet to unsettled levels.

.Forecast...
Quiet to active conditions are expected through most of 26 Nov. Isolated
G1 (Minor) geomagnetic storms are likely by late on 26 Nov and
continuing into 27 Nov, with 28 Nov returning to generally unsettled to
active conditions.
