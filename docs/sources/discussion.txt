Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity continued at very low levels with only B-level
enhancements observed. Region 4579 (N15W36, Hsx/alpha) remained
unchanged this period while Region 4581 (S08W01, Dro/beta) exhibited
minor decay, especially amongst the trailing spots. An area of
rudimentary spots were intermittently observed near S10W27, but this
area remained unnumbered at the time of writing.

No Earth-directed CMEs were identified in the available coronagraph
imagery during the period.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be at very low levels, with a chance for
C-class flares and a slight chance for M-class flares (R1-R2,
Minor-Moderate) on 23-25 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux remained at moderate levels. The
greater than 10 MeV proton flux continued at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to be at normal to
moderate levels on 23-25 Nov. The greater than 10 MeV proton flux is
expected to continue at background levels on through 25 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters became weakly enhanced after ~22/0630 UTC,
possibly associated with a SSBC. Total field increased to 8 nT and Bz
saw a simultaneous southward deflection to -6 nT, and wind speeds
increased to around 400 km/s.

.Forecast...
Slightly enhanced solar wind conditions are possible on 23 Nov due to
the anticipated glancing influence from the 19 Nov CME. Ambient solar
wind conditions are expected to prevail on 24-25 Nov.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
Geomagnetic field activity was at quiet levels throughout the period.

.Forecast...
The geomagnetic field is expected to be at quiet to active levels on 23
Nov due to possible glancing CME influences. Mostly quiet conditions are
expected to prevail on 24-25 Nov.
