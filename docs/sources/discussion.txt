Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity reached moderate (R1-Minor) levels following an M2.4
flare off the WSW limb at 19/1324 UTC and an M1.1 flare from Region 4572
(N11W70, Axx/alpha) at 19/1359 UTC. Late in the period, Region 4579
(N16W02, Hsx/alpha) added a C9.6 flare that peaked at 20/1130 UTC.
Regions 4572, 4574 (N16W51, Axx/alpha), 4577 (S06W66, Axx/alpha), and
4581 (S07E14, Dso/beta) all contributed low-level C-class flares during
the past 24 hours, despite reflecting either stagnant or decaying
trends.

Modeling of the previously discussed eruption was attempted with
questionable results, at best. With only a handful of available/useful
coronagraph images, analysis indicated a possible arrival at Earth just
outside this forecast period. Confidence is low with this forecast.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is likely to be at low levels, with a slight chance for
M-class flares (R1-R2, Minor-Moderate) on 20-22 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached moderate levels. The
greater than 10 MeV proton flux was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to be at moderate
levels on 20-22 Nov. The greater than 10 MeV proton flux is expected to
continue at background levels on through 22 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters were near background levels. Total field (Bt)
averaged 4-8 nT, while the Bz component saw southward deflections
reaching -6 nT early before turning northward by the end of the period.
Solar wind speeds gradually decreased from ~470-500 km/s to around 390
km/s. Phi was predominantly positive through the period.

.Forecast...
Continued background conditions are likely over 20 Nov - 22 Nov. Slight
enhancements are possible during the period if the weakened, nearly
closed, negative polarity CH HSS moves into a geoeffective position.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic field was quiet to unsettled.

.Forecast...
Primarily quiet to unsettled conditions are expected through 21 Nov,
with a chance for isolated active periods due to coronal hole
influences. Unsettled to active periods are expected on 22 Nov with
additional coronal hole and possible CME influences.
