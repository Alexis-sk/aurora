Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity continued at very low levels with only B-level
enhancements. Regions 4579 (N15W70, Hsx/alpha) and 4581 (S07W40,
Hrx/alpha) remained relatively stable while Region 4582 (N19E44,
Hrx/alpha) underwent decay in its trailing spots. No Earth-directed CMEs
were observed in available coronagraph imagery.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be at very low levels, with a slight
chance for C-class flares on 25-27 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux remained at moderate levels. The
greater than 10 MeV proton flux continued at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to be at normal to
moderate levels on 25-27 Nov. The greater than 10 MeV proton flux is
expected to continue at background levels through 27 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected solar sector boundary crossings and
possibly early CIR influences. Total field increased from ambient-like
levels to peaks of 7-8 nT and the Bz component underwent a few southward
deflections reaching -6 nT. Solar wind speeds underwent a slight
increase from ~310 km/s to ~375 km/s by the end of the reporting period.

.Forecast...
Slightly disturbed solar wind conditions are anticipated to continue
through late on 26 Nov as a recurrent positive polarity coronal hole
high speed stream (CH HSS) becomes geoeffective brining fast solar wind.
CH HSS influences are expected to then persist through 27 Nov.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
Geomagnetic field activity was at quiet to unsettled levels.

.Forecast...
Quiet to active conditions are expected for the remainder of 25 Nov and
through most of 26 Nov. G1 (Minor) geomagnetic storms are likely by late
on 26 Nov and continuing into 27 Nov.
