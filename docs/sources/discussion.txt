Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity was at low levels today. Region 4566 (S08W62,
Eai/beta-gamma) was responsible for the majority of the C-class flares.
The largest was a C7.0 flare at 12/0344 UTC. Region 4566 continued to
decay as it approached the western limb. New Region 4576 (S18W12,
Bxo/beta) was numbered.

No Earth-directed CMEs were observed in available coronagraph imagery.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be low with a chance for M-class flares
(R1-R2, Minor-Moderate) over 13-15 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels with a peak
flux of 3,235 pfu observed at 12/1515 UTC. The greater than 10 MeV
proton flux was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to reach high levels
over 13-14 Nov. A return to moderate levels is likely on 15 Nov. The
greater than 10 MeV proton flux is expected to continue at background
levels on 13-15 Nov.

Solar Wind
