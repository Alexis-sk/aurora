Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity remained at low levels, with the largest flare of the
period a C5.4/1f at 16/0658 UTC from Region 4579 (N15E46, Hhx/alpha).
However, Region 4578 (S12W59, Cri/beta) was the main flare factory of
the reporting period as it exhibited consistent flux emergence in its
intermediary region. Region 4576 (S19W67, Dao/beta), just south of 4578,
also exhibited some growth but didnt produce any flaring activity.
Regions 4572 (N13W24, Cro/beta) and 4577 (S07W24, Dro/beta) had spots
re-emerging during the reported period, while the remaining regions were
stable or in slight decay.

At approximately 16/0439 UTC, an estimated eight degree long filament,
centered near N19W57, was observed lifting off the visible disk in GONG
H-alpha imagery. An associated CME first became visible in LASCO C2 at
16/0524 UTC. Modeling estimated a very slow velocity (<200 km/s) with
the majority of the bulk directed ahead of Earths orbit. A possible
glancing blow cannot necessarily be ruled out on 22-23 Nov, however
confidence is low.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be low with a chance for M-class flares
(R1-R2, Minor-Moderate) over 17-19 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels with a peak
flux of 4,744 pfu observed at 16/1500 UTC. The greater than 10 MeV
proton flux was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to reach moderate
levels on 17-18 Nov, with an anticipated return to high levels on 19
Nov. The greater than 10 MeV proton flux is expected to continue at
background levels on 17-19 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected a background solar wind regime______.
Total magnetic field (Bt) ranged from 2-5 nT, with largely neutral Bz
(north-south) component. Solar wind speed continued its gradual decline
to end of period speeds under 300 km/s. Phi was in a predominantly
negative (towards the Sun) orientation.

.Forecast...
Weak enhancements in solar wind parameters are likely on 17-18 Nov as a
positive polarity CH HSS moves into a geoeffective position, coupled
with the possible arrival of the 14 Nov CME late on 17 Nov into early 18
Nov. These enhancements are anticipated to begin waning through 19 Nov.
