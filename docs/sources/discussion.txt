# Prepared by the U.S. Dept. of Commerce, NOAA, Space Weather Prediction Center

Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity continued at very low levels with only B-level
enhancements, the majority of which came from beyond the eastern limb
just south of the equator. Region 4582 (N18, L=241) fully decayed to
plage. No Earth-directed CMEs were observed in available coronagraph
imagery.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be at very low levels, with a slight
chance for C-class flares on 27-29 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux remained at moderate levels. The
greater than 10 MeV proton flux continued at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to be at normal to
moderate levels on 27 Nov, and then reaching high levels on 28-29 Nov.
The greater than 10 MeV proton flux is expected to continue at
background levels through 29 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters largely reflected background conditions. Total
magnetic field (Bt) ranged from 3 to 6 nT, while the Bz component ranged
from +3/-5 nT. Solar wind speeds gradually decreased throughout the
period from ~350 km/s to ~295 km/s. Phi was predominantly positive (away
from the Sun).

.Forecast...
Enhanced solar wind conditions due to positive polarity CH HSS
influences are expected on 27-28 Nov, with 29 Nov largely returning to
background conditions.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
Geomagnetic field activity was quiet.

.Forecast...
Isolated G1 (Minor) geomagnetic storms are likely on 27 Nov and
unsettled to active conditions on 28 Nov due to the positive polarity CH
HSS moving through near-Earth space. 29 Nov is expected to see a
returned to quiet conditions.
