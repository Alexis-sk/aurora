Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity continued at very low levels with only B-level
enhancements observed. There are only two active regions with spots on
the visible disk: Region 4579 (N14W30, Hsx/alpha) and Region 4581
(S09E05, Dso/beta). Region 4579 lost most of its peripheral spots around
the main leader spot, while Region 4581 lost spots in both the leader
and trailer areas. An area of development was noted near S09W51 and will
be monitored for possible numbering.

No Earth-directed CMEs were identified in the available coronagraph
imagery during the period.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be at very low levels, with a chance for
C-class flares and a slight chance for M-class flares (R1-R2,
Minor-Moderate) on 22-24 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux remained at moderate levels. The
greater than 10 MeV proton flux continued at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to be at moderate
levels on 22-24 Nov. The greater than 10 MeV proton flux is expected to
continue at background levels on through 24 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters continued to reflect a background solar wind
regime, with total field fluctuating between 2-5 nT and the Bz component
oscillating between +/- 3 nT for most of the period. The solar wind
speed was around 330-350 km/s during most of the period and Phi was
predominately positive. A weak enhancement, possibly associated with a
SSBC, was observed at around 22/0630 UTC when total field increased to 8
nT, Bz saw a simultaneous southward deflection to -6 nT, wind speeds
increased slightly to around 390 km/s, and Phi rotated into a negative
orientation.

.Forecast...
Nominal solar wind conditions are likely to continue on 22 and 24 Nov,
with a slight and short-lived disturbance possible on 23 Nov due to a
low confidence glancing blow of CME from 19 Nov.

Geomagnetic activity has been quiet.

.Forecast...
Quiet geomagnetic conditions are expected on 22 Nov and 24 Nov. Quiet to

G1 (Minor) storming period due to the possible CME glancing influences.
