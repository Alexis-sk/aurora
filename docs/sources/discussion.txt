Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity was at low levels. While Regions 4574 (N17W00, Dai/beta)
and 4575 (S10E05, Hsx/alpha) were the most active, producing the
majority of the low-level B- and C-class flares, Region 4579 (N15E52,
Hsx/alpha) spawned the largest of the period: a C5.4/1f at 16/0658 UTC.
Regions 4574 and 4576 (S18W59, Dao/beta) both experienced growth in
their leader spots, while the remaining regions were unchanged or
exhibited slight decay.

At approximately 16/0439 UTC, an estimated eight degree long filament,
centered near N19W57, was observed lifting off the visible disk in GONG
H-alpha imagery. Analysis of the resulting CME is currently underway.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be low with a chance for M-class flares
(R1-R2, Minor-Moderate) over 16-18 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels with a peak
flux of 3,378 pfu observed at 15/1405 UTC. The greater than 10 MeV
proton flux was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to reach high levels on
16 Nov and decrease to moderate levels on 17-18 Nov. The greater than 10
MeV proton flux is expected to continue at background levels on 16-18
Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected a background solar wind regime. Total
field ranged from 2-4 nT, the Bz component was variable between +/-3 nT,
and solar wind speed continued its gradual decline to end of period
speeds under 300 km/s. Phi was in a mostly negative orientation.

.Forecast...
Nominal solar wind conditions are expected on 16 Nov. Weak enhancements
in solar wind parameters are likely on 17-18 Nov as a positive polarity
CH HSS moves into a geoeffective position, coupled with the possible
arrival of the 14 Nov CME late on 17 Nov into early 18 Nov.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic field was quiet.

.Forecast...
Mostly quiet conditions are expected early on 16 Nov, increasing to
unsettled to isolated active periods late in the day. A further increase
to active to isolated G1 (Minor) levels is likely on 17-18 Nov as the CH
HSS moves into a geoeffective position along with the possible arrival
of the 14 Nov CME late in the period.
