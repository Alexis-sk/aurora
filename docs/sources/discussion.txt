Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity reached moderate levels. Region 4566 (S06E26,
Dac/beta-gamma-delta) produced an M3.5/2b flare at 05/1833 UTC.
Associated with the event was a Type II radio sweep with an estimated
velocity of 609 km/s. Regions 4564 (N01W33, Bxo/beta), 4565 (N13W11,
Cso/beta), 4567 (N18W63, Cso/beta) exhibited minor decay while the
remaining regions were mostly stable. New Regions 4569 (S06E14,
Axx/alpha) and 4570 (S13E66, Axx/alpha) were numbered, but were
otherwise unremarkable. No Earth-directed CMEs were detected in
available coronagraph imagery.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be at low levels, with a chance for
M-class flares (R1-R2/Minor-Moderate) and a slight chance for an X-class
flare (R3/Strong or greater) on 06-08 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels with a peak
flux of 2,197 pfu observed at 05/1700 UTC. The greater than 10 MeV
proton flux remained at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to reach high levels on
06 Nov, with normal to moderate levels expected on 07-08 Nov. The
greater than 10 MeV proton flux is expected to remain at background
levels through 08 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected the initial arrival of CIR effects
preceding negative polarity CH HSS influences. Total magnetic field
strength reached 9 nT, and the Bz component varied between +6/-8 nT.
Solar wind speeds ranged between ~300-350 km/s. Phi was highly variable
throughout much of the period.

.Forecast...
Enhanced solar wind conditions are expected to prevail on 06-08 Nov due
to negative polarity CH HSS influences.
