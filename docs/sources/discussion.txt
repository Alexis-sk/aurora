Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity was very low in the past 24h. Region 4566 (S07E66,
Cro/beta) was responsible for all the B-class flares of the period, with
the largest one being a B7.7 at 02/0608 UTC. The other two active
regions on disk, Region 4564 (N03E11, Hrx/alpha) and Region 4565
(N13E35, Hsx/alpha), were decaying or stable, respectively.

A filament erupted around 01/1800 UTC and was associated with a CME
observed propagating away from the W-limb in the first hours of the 02
Nov UTC day. Analysis of this CME indicates a weak glancing blow
near-Earth late on 05 Nov. No other Earth-directed CMEs were observed in
the available coronagraph imagery.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be very low with a chance for C-class
flares over 05 Nov.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels with a peak
flux of 4,480 pfu at 02/1540 UTC. The greater than 10 MeV proton flux
was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to continue reaching
high levels through 05 Nov. The greater than 10 MeV proton flux is
expected to remain at background levels through 05 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters continued reflecting waning positive polarity CH
HSS influences. Total interplanetary magnetic field (IMF) strength was
around 5 nT and the Bz (north-south) component oscillating between +/- 4
nT. Solar wind speeds slowly decreased from 450 km/s levels down to 380
km/s at the end of the period. Phi angle oscillated during the day,
settling in the positive sector (away from the Sun) after about 02/1800
UTC.

.Forecast...
The solar wind near-Earth is expected to reach undisturbed conditions on
03-04 Nov, as the ongoing positive polarity CH HSS influences diminish.
Mild disturbances are anticipated on 05 Nov, as a small positive
polarity CH HSS becomes geoeffective and a weak glancing blow from 01
Nov CME is possible.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic activity was at quiet to unsettled levels in the past
24h due to waning positive polarity CH HSS influences.

.Forecast...
The geomagnetic field activity is expected to be mostly at quiet levels
on 03-04 Nov as the ongoing positive polarity CH HSS influences vanish.
Unsettled to active conditions are anticipated on 05 Nov as a response
to influences of a small positive polarity CH HSS and the 01 Nov CME
glancing blow near Earth.
